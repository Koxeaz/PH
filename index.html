Welcome to the Philippine Archipelago

With 7,641 islands, the Philippines offers world-famous white beaches, majestic active volcanoes, surfing hotspots, unique geographical landscapes, and warm hospitality. Explore five top destinations below complete with their famous spots, dishes, cultural traditions, working YouTube videos & audio, and guides on how to book your trip.

1. Boracay Island, Aklan

🏝️ Tropical Paradise
📍 Location: Malay, Aklan Province, Western Visayas (Region VI)

Boracay is a world-renowned tropical island celebrated for its four-kilometer stretch of White Beach, turquoise waters, vibrant nightlife, and powder-fine sand.

📍 Famous Spot

Willy's Rock & Puka Shell Beach — Iconic volcanic rock formations and quiet northern shores.

🍽️ Local Delicacies

Chori Burger (sweet chorizo patty on a grilled bun) and refreshing Calamansi Muffins.

🏄 Activities & Traditions

Sunset Paraw sailing, scuba diving, cliff jumping at Ariel's Point, and witnessing the annual Ati-Atihan festive dance roots.

📞 How to Book & Contact

Fly to Caticlan Airport (MPH). Register via the official Malay Tourism Portal or book through accredited hotels via philippines.travel.

2. Mayon Volcano, Albay (Bicol)

🌋 Majestic Cone
📍 Location: Albay Province, Bicol Region (Region V), Luzon

Standing at over 2,400 meters, Mayon Volcano is world-famous for its symmetrical cone shape and rich Bicolano heritage.

📍 Famous Spot

Cagsawa Ruins — The historic 18th-century church belfry with Mayon in the backdrop, and Sumlang Lake.

🍽️ Local Delicacies

Bicol Express (pork cooked in chili and coconut milk), Laing, and spicy Sagoniton Chili Ice Cream.

🏄 Activities & Traditions

Thrilling ATV lava-trail rides, ziplining, and celebrating the annual Magayon Festival honoring local folklore.

📞 How to Book & Contact

Book Mayon ATV tours through official operators like Cagsawa ATV Adventures or via Klook / Albay Provincial Tourism (+63 956 563 1816).

3. La Union (Elyu)

🌊 Surf Capital North
📍 Location: San Juan, La Union, Ilocos Region (Region I), Northwestern Luzon

La Union (Elyu) is a coastal haven that combines surf culture, artsy beachside cafes, and vibrant sunset gatherings.

📍 Famous Spot

Mona Liza Point in San Juan for surfing, and the peaceful Tangadan Falls in San Gabriel.

🍽️ Local Delicacies

Ilocano Bagnet, Dinakdakan, and trendy surf-side coffee specialty brews.

🏄 Activities & Traditions

Beginner-to-pro surfing lessons, beach volleyball, cafe hopping, and honoring coastal craft traditions.

📞 How to Book & Contact

Drive 4–5 hours from Manila via TPLEX or take Partas Bus. Contact La Union Surf Club (LUSC) for lesson reservations.

4. Siargao Island

🏄 Wave & Island Hub
📍 Location: Surigao del Norte, Caraga Region (Region XIII), Mindanao (Facing the Pacific Ocean)

Known as the Surfing Capital of the Philippines, Siargao is world-renowned for its teardrop shape, coconut tree forests, and natural rock pools.

📍 Famous Spot

Cloud 9 Boardwalk, Magpupungko Rock Pools, and Sugba Lagoon.

🍽️ Local Delicacies

Fresh seafood platters, Coconut Smoothie Bowls, and traditional Pan de Sayao.

🏄 Activities & Traditions

Renting a scooter down Coconut Road, island hopping to Naked, Daku, and Guyam Islands, and attending local surf festivals.

📞 How to Book & Contact

Fly directly to Sayak Airport (IAO). Contact Siargao Tourism Office or book land tours via local cooperative guides.

5. Chocolate Hills, Bohol

⛰️ Natural Wonder
📍 Location: Bohol Province, Central Visayas (Region VII) — Mainly in Carmen, Batuan, and Sagbayan

A geological wonder consisting of over 1,200 symmetrical mounds that turn chocolate brown during the dry summer months.

📍 Famous Spot

Carmen Observation Deck and the nearby Loboc River Cruise & Tarsier Sanctuary.

🍽️ Local Delicacies

Kalamay (sweet sticky rice dessert in coconut shell), Peanut Kisses, and Chorizo de Bohol.

🏄 Activities & Traditions

ATV riding around the base of the hills, zip-biking at Chocolate Hills Complex, and watching traditional Sandugo Festival re-enactments.

📞 How to Book & Contact

Fly into Panglao International Airport (TAG). Book day tours through Bohol Provincial Tourism Office or accredited local agencies.
